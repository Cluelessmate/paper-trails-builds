# Paper Trails — test builds

A tiny pixel writer lives on your desktop, behind your windows, and folds paper planes that fly
to real people. This repo only hosts the Windows test builds; the game's source is private.

## Download

Get the latest zip from **[Releases](../../releases/latest)**.

1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere (e.g. `Documents\PaperTrails`). Keep `pt_helper.exe` next to
   `PaperTrails.exe`.
2. Run `PaperTrails.exe`. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the game isn't
   signed yet: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. The desk appears at the bottom of your screen. A short tour shows you around.

## Playing

- Click the paper stack to write a plane. It lands on another player's desk.
- Planes from others land along the bottom of your screen. Click one to open it, then reply.
- Once you've both written, you're pen pals: right-click the desk > **Pen pals**.
- Everything else (stars, pets, props, rugs, lamps, the radio) is on your own computer.

The `README.txt` in the zip has more. Be kind: letters go to real people.

## Problems?

Right-click the desk > **Settings** > **Connection** > **Copy a problem report**, and send it to
whoever gave you the game. Your save lives in `%APPDATA%\PaperTrails`.

When a new version is out, the game tells you, and **Get the new version** opens this page.
