# Paper Trails privacy policy

_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

Paper Trails is a desktop game in which you write letters and send them as paper planes to
other players. This page explains what the game stores, who can see it, how long it is kept and
how to delete it. Paper Trails has no ads and no analytics, and it never sells or shares your data.

## What other players see

When someone receives your letter, they see:

- the letter itself: the text, your drawing, your stickers, and the plane and paper you picked
- the date it was sent
- your display name, but only if you set one and turn off "anonymous". By default you are
  anonymous and no name is shown.
- your broad region (for example "Europe"), but only if you turn that on

Nobody ever sees your Steam name, Steam ID, IP address or exact location.

## What the server stores

The game uses a server (hosted by [Supabase](https://supabase.com)) only to carry letters
between players. It stores:

| What | Why | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your Steam ID (or, in test builds, a random ID made by the game) | To sign you in. It is never shown to anyone. | Until you delete your account |
| Display name, "anonymous" choice, broad region and whether to share it | To show what you chose on your letters | Until you change it or delete your account |
| Who may send to you (everyone, friends or nobody), whether you accept drawings, your letter languages | To deliver the right letters to you | Until you delete your account |
| When you were last online | To send random planes to people who are actually playing | Until you delete your account |
| The letters you send, and whether each one was delivered and opened | To deliver letters to players who are offline, and to tell you when yours is opened | Deleted after 60 days |
| The players you block and the conversations you mute | So their letters stop reaching you | Until you remove them or delete your account |
| Your Steam friends who also play | So friends can send planes to each other. A friendship only counts when both of you have each other in your friends list. | Updated each time you play |
| Reports, including a copy of the reported letter | So a moderator can review it | Up to 180 days |

Your scrapbook, settings, Paper Stars and unlocks are stored only on your computer, at
`%APPDATA%\PaperTrails`. They are also in Steam Cloud, if you have it turned on.

The server also handles each connection's IP address, as every internet service does. The
game does not record it.

## Safety and moderation

- Letters containing links, email addresses, phone numbers, social media handles or words on
  our block list are bounced before they are sent.
- You can report any letter. Reporting hides it from you straight away. If several different
  players report the same sender, that sender's planes are held until a moderator reviews them.
  A moderator can suspend senders who break the rules.
- You can block a sender, mute a conversation, stop accepting drawings, or receive letters only
  from friends (or from nobody).

## Deleting your data

Go to **Settings → Privacy & data → Delete my account**. This deletes your account from the
server, along with every stored letter you have sent, your blocks and preferences, and all
letters on your computer. Letters that other players have already received stay on their
computers, because those are their copies.

## Children

Paper Trails is not aimed at children under 13, and you must meet Steam's age requirements to
play it. If you think a child has sent us their personal details, contact us and we will delete them.
